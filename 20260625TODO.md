## Pilot Smoke Checklist

A pilot tester must be able to:

1. Create/select an organisation.
2. Complete go-live checklist basics.
3. Confirm chart of accounts and bank accounts.
4. Enter/import opening balances.
5. Upload or process supplier invoices/receipts.
6. Upload or process bank statement lines.
7. Review and post bank journals.
8. Reconcile supplier statement/payment activity.
9. Run a supplier payment run, generate remittances, mark paid, and reconcile the payment.
10. Create/send or export a customer statement.
11. Review collections.
12. Produce Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, VAT, Aged AP, Aged AR, Cash Flow, and General Ledger.
13. Close/lock a period and verify locked posting protection.
14. Review audit trail.
15. Export data required for an accountant/auditor.
